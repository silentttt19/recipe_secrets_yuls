About Us

Our Story

Thirty years of cooking from one worn notebook.

Since 1994

A notebook, a small kitchen, a big idea

Recipe Secrets opened in 1994 with eight tables and a stained notebook that belonged to our founder's grandmother. Every page was a dish, and every dish had a note in the margin: the little thing that made it hers.

Today the dining room is bigger, but the notebook still sits by the pass. We cook from it every night, adding only what the seasons and our local farmers bring to the door.

Philosophy

The Secret Behind the Secrets

We guard our recipes, but we'll share what they stand for.

Patience

Stocks simmer for 18 hours. Doughs rest overnight. Good flavour can't be hurried, and we don't try.

Provenance

We know our growers by name. If it isn't in season or from a farm we trust, it isn't on your plate.

Generosity

Big flavours, generous portions and a welcome that makes strangers feel like regulars.
